Key for the Box keeps one pretend box on the phone. The key starts on the hook and the lid is shut. Put the key in before the lid will open. The key stays in while the lid is up. Shut the lid, then take the key out. The huge line says what the box is doing.

The box stays on this phone. Start a new box when you want the key on the hook again.

Key for the Box does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, a lock code, or a network connection. It does not open a real box. The box in the app is original.
